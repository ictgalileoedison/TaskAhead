About TaskAhead

TaskAhead Platform Framework

TaskAhead is a specialized, production-ready schoolwork workflow management application. Designed around the intensive track challenges of modern Senior High School environments, it pairs academic tracking with a cozy, garden-fairy interface that adapts across devices.

Credit Notice & Research Attribution (ctto):
This application was researched, conceptualized, and built by Anonang National High School Students as a primary practical development solution for their formal research track study. Engineered to boost focus for ICT student tracking, its versatile design extends complete functional utility to multiple general tracks and student cohorts across institutions.
